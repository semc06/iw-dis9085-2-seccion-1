    ABOUT

    ROBERT WUN

    Is a London based fashion label. Presenting storytelling collections through modern artistry and craftsmanship, exploring narratives of visibility and liberation in fashion. Born in Hong Kong, Robert was discovered by Joyce Boutique for his graduate collection at the London College of Fashion in 2012. After working 2 years as a freelancer in the industry, Robert then launched his label in 2014, based in London, focused on custom order only collections, with a fresh take on shoes and accessories, alongside his unique tailoring and inspirations drawn from movies and the world of nature. The brand debuted its first runway show in Paris, January 2023, with the support of Bruno Pavlovsky, President of Fashion at CHANEL. Robert became the first designer from Hong Kong to join the Haute Couture Calendar in its history, as a guest member of The Fédération de la Haute Couture et de la Mode. obert was the Prix Spécial Winner of the ANDAM Fashion Awards 2022. He was also named one of the most influential individuals in the fashion industry by Business of Fashion 500, Vogue Business 100 and Tatler Asia, he was nominated by Wallpaper China as Designer of the Year, Elle China’s Special Designer and also nominated for New Establishments Womenswear - The Fashion Awards 2023 by the British Fashion Council. Having dressed and worked with artists like Beyoncé, Celine Dion, Lady Gaga, Adele, Cardi B, Florence Pugh, Cate Blanchett, Usher and Idris Elba. The designer was also commissioned and has worked with The Royal Ballet in London, the Hunger Games Movie Franchise and the renowned Movie Director Wong Kar Wai.
